INKBOUND — Storyboard
Assumptions: a lightless world where your glowing ink is the only thing holding back the dark — and it doesn't last. Ink only becomes a platform once both ends bite into solid ground; drawing into the air does nothing. A 5-segment meter caps each draw; wells refill it. Drawn ink rots away seconds after it's placed, so routes are temporary and something is always a step behind you. Tone: quiet dread with a wry, unsettled narrator. Below: title, level select, self-talk dialogue, settings, and the cave gameplay beat — our chosen direction — before wiring up real physics.
